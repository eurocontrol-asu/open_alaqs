changelog=5.3.1
    Bug-fix release. Computed results change for studies with trajectories crossing the vertical limit.
    Fixed: trajectory segments crossing the vertical limit (mixing height) now count only their part below it, in the plugin, the standalone and the CAEP14 reference (previously the whole segment was counted; departures most affected). Helicopter movements of different types sharing a profile, runway and taxi route no longer receive the first member's emissions. Movement names and log lines show the input (local) time instead of being shifted by the machine's UTC offset. Standalone: engine lookup keys on engine_name as the plugin does; BFFM2 segments without power and fuel flow use Bymode indices as the plugin does.
    Tests: ANP expected CSV regenerated; new regression tests for each fix.
    See CHANGELOG.md for details.
